Hi, I'm Arif.

I work in two dimensions and three — building scenes in Blender, then drawing over the edges of what a mesh can't say.

Huion tablet

Where the render stops being enough — texture, line, and color pass by hand.

Most of what's here started as a question I couldn't answer in one medium alone. A 3D block-out that needed a hand-drawn pass to feel finished, or a sketch that needed depth a flat page couldn't give it.
